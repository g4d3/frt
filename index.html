What this does

      
It records where and when a febrile traveller stayed and what they were exposed to, then lists the infections that are possible for those places and exposures, with incubation periods checked against the fever-onset date. It deliberately ignores symptoms: it is the start of the diagnostic process, not a differential-diagnosis engine.

      
Limits

      
    
        
  • Geography is country-level, except where a mapped risk area has been drawn (choose Mapped areas above the map: e.g. valley fever in the south-west USA, malaria in Kruger or the Thai border provinces). In those countries a pin outside the area does not pick up that risk, and the report lists it under "Not at this pin". The outlines are rough: always check the area visited on NaTHNaC or the CDC maps.
    • 
        
  • Outbreaks change weekly. The alerts list is maintained by hand from NaTHNaC and UKHSA Outbreaks under monitoring; alerts past their review date are flagged as stale.
    • 
        
  • It is not a substitute for the ACDP VHF risk assessment, local guidance or specialist advice. Imported Fever Service: 0844 778 8990 (24/7).
